General Information

From the Spanish Society of Chemometrics and Qualimetrics (SEQyC), we are pleased to announce the
VII Workshop of Young Researchers in Chemometrics,
which will be held on January 22–23, 2026 at the Martina Casiano Building, Leioa Campus of the UPV/EHU.
JIQ is a training and scientific exchange space for young people in the field of chemometrics.
The provisional program includes:

  • Morning training sessions:
    Dr. Davide Ballabio (Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca, Italy) will cover introductory and advanced aspects of classification analysis.
  • Afternoon oral presentations:
    Young researchers will present their work in short oral communications (10–15 minutes). Depending on attendance, a poster session will also be organized.
  • Possible poster session:
    Depending on participation, a poster session for the presentation of works will also be held.
  • Important dates:
    • December 10, 2025: Deadline for abstract submission and registration (with contributions).
    January 10, 2026: Final deadline for registration (without contributions).
